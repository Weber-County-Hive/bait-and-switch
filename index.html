Short version
  • 19 cases, 2017 to 2026, in four parts. Seventeen cases are about Utah bills; two are from Congress.
  • Each case puts a bill's first version next to its final version and grades the change on power and transparency.
  • One case (Case 12) changed in the public's favor. It is included for balance.
  • Part 4 explains that 471 unfinished bills were closed out at 11:59 p.m. on March 6, 2026, the normal way a session ends.
